13. Immutable Records: Limits on Correction and Deletion

Bearing and Cadence's core function is to produce a tamper-evident, litigation-grade record. Once a daily log entry, photo, transcription, or audit-trail entry is created, it cannot be altered or deleted through the application — this is enforced at the database level, not merely a policy choice, and it is the mechanism the product's reliability depends on.

This has the following practical effects, which we state here directly rather than implying a blanket right that does not exist:

  • Correction: an immutable entry cannot be edited. Instead, a correction is made by adding a new, linked addendum entry that supplements the original and is itself visible in the record — preserving both the original entry and the correction, consistent with how a chain-of-custody record is properly maintained.
  • Deletion: an immutable entry cannot be deleted on request. It is retained for the period specified in §9 and disposed of only when that period expires, except where a litigation hold or legal obligation requires longer retention.
  • Deletable data: account data, session data, and mutable profile fields (Tiers 1–2, company profile, marketing contact records) are not subject to this limitation and can be corrected or deleted as described in §11.

If you submit a correction or deletion request that touches an immutable record, we will process the deletable portions of your request and explain this limitation with respect to the rest, rather than silently declining or silently complying in a way that would compromise record integrity.
